## Key findings from the 2017 Second Amendment

1. **FMV reset already exists.** Section 6: the 3 existing 5-yr options reset to Fair Market Value Rent at each renewal, **floored at the rent then in effect**, with an MAI appraisal procedure (each side appoints; average if within 10%; third appraiser otherwise). Section 6(c) voided all older options. → The "no-reset options" risk is a **drafting risk, not a negotiation risk**: new options must incorporate Section 6 terms by reference.
2. **Hard deadline working for us:** renewal notice due 9 months pre-expiry (passed ~5/31/26); absolute cutoff **6 months pre-expiry ≈ Aug 31, 2026**. After that Hamilton's fallback (exercise existing option) expires. Landlord has a notice duty re: missed deadline — handle formally with counsel.
3. **TI anchor:** 2017 deal gave $225K improvement rent credit (24 × $9,375, capped at demonstrated costs within 3 yrs, last 2 installments held for completion + lien waivers) + $25K elevator. Our current $150K + $75K = same $225K total. Expect their ask to grow toward/past $225K once architects price the 2nd floor.
4. **Approvals precedent:** 2017 Improvements (Witherspoon St entrance, Historic Preservation Commission) were tenant's sole responsibility — approvals, permits, cost. Direct template for putting the 2nd-floor office→retail variance on Hamilton.
5. **Elevator precedent:** landlord maintains elevator at landlord's cost **chargeable through Operating Expenses** — the existing model for landlord-maintained equipment under NNN. Use against the $4K trigger.
6. Management fee during any Renewal Term = 3% of fixed + additional rent. Carry forward.
